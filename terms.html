Meal values and configurable scoring

The simplified Meal value field is the value of food and drink attributable to the user, not the whole table’s bill. If the user marks that someone paid for the meal, Recolleat records no personal spending for that meal. Values and derived financial summaries are self-reported and should not be treated as accounting or payment records.

Location photos can be selected locally or copied from outside sources. The Google Images shortcut opens an external search; you remain responsible for selecting images you have permission to use, particularly if you share or publicly publish them. Recolleat does not provide an image licence or automatically download Google Images results.

Ranking weights can be changed globally in Settings; this changes rankings and comparisons without changing the stored original ratings. Previous dish-category information is retained for compatibility even when category books are not visible.
