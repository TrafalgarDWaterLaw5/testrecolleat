Frequently asked questions

Does Recolleat need an account or internet connection?

No account is needed for the journal. Optional accounts support accepted friends and deliberate sharing of selected restaurant details. Saved entries work locally on this device. Initial installation and updates require internet access; your saved journal works offline.

How is a restaurant's overall score calculated?

By default, food contributes 60%, value 20% and service 20%. You can change global scoring weights under More → Settings. Scores and rankings update without changing the underlying restaurant ratings.

Where is my Palate Code, and why are some letters unknown?

Open My Profile from the Hub. Your four letters come from rated dishes and restaurant context. Price preference uses distinct priced restaurants and compares whether higher-priced meals tend to receive higher food ratings. A balanced result is shown as ≈ rather than inventing a preference. The maturity bar measures data coverage, not statistical certainty. The 16 possible personas are explained under World of Personas.

What does Palate Code measure?

Palate Code uses your rated dishes and, for its spending dimension, the recorded cost per person or food value of your visits. The first dimensions describe cuisine breadth and repeated dish names; the others compare food ratings with price and measure rating generosity. The price trait uses rank association and compares lower-priced versus higher-priced rated restaurants, avoiding assumptions when prices are missing. It does not use a questionnaire or treat the number of visits as evidence of personality. Ratings from the same restaurant are averaged so a long menu does not dominate. A meaningful price spread is required: at least £10 or 20% of median cost, whichever is greater, between the lower and upper half medians. Typical cost is shown separately from rating preference. Near-neutral relationships can remain unassigned; the code may evolve as you add meals.

How do I add a location picture?

Open Places and tap Add photo or Edit photo on a location card. Select Choose from gallery, or use the other picture options. Search Google Images opens a separate Google search using the location name; choose a picture you have the right to use, save it to your phone or computer, then upload it. More picture options supports pasting a copied image on compatible browsers. All selected location pictures are stored locally and shared by restaurant cards with the same location label. Online searches send the search query to Google.

How do I scan a receipt?

Open Scan receipt from Home or Quick Log. Photograph a receipt. On supported browsers the app reads its text on-device. Where this is unavailable, copy the receipt text using your phone's text-recognition feature and paste it into the editor. Select the items you ate, correct prices, and transfer them into a Quick Log. Recolleat does not automatically upload or share receipts. Other applications used to copy text may have their own privacy terms.

How do I view the two sides of a restaurant passport?

Open any restaurant card, then switch between Ratings and Journal. On a phone you can also swipe horizontally over the passport page. The Journal side contains visit history, notes, dishes, photographs and location.

How do I record a visit without knowing the date?

Open the restaurant editor and add a visit with the date left blank. It counts as logged, but is excluded from month-specific charts.

Why do I see “10+” visits?

Restaurant visit badges show 10+ after ten visits. Recolleat retains the exact number internally. Your profile only counts actual logged visit records.

How do Quick Add and drafts work?

Tap Add to record a restaurant and its visit together. A food score is required to save a completed meal. Add multiple dishes and photographs immediately, or open More details for your meal value, cuisine and notes. Existing unfinished drafts remain available for editing and backup.

How do I tell different branches apart?

Enter the precise branch name and neighbourhood. Each restaurant record remains independently editable.

How do I move my journal to another phone?

Export a JSON backup from the Hub, install Recolleat at the same website address on the new device, then import the backup. Changes do not synchronise automatically.

How do I install Recolleat on my phone?

On iPhone, open the website in Safari and use Share → Add to Home Screen. On other phones, open the site in Chrome and choose Add to Home screen or Install app. Open once while connected to the internet to initialise offline caching.

Where are my photos and restaurant details stored?

In browser storage on this device, not in a Recolleat cloud account. Clearing site data or removing the app can delete them, so export backups regularly.

How do I unlock more colour themes?

Open More → Colour themes. Recolleat Classic and Black & White are available immediately. Plum & Champagne unlocks at 10 saved restaurants, Electric Citrus at 20, Crimson Red at 30 and Golden Hour at 40. These are different saved restaurants, not visits, dishes or unfinished drafts. Your existing or imported saved restaurants count. Earned theme rewards are permanent, even after deleting an entry. All milestones stay on this device with your collection; no account is needed.

Can I change the colours and switch dark mode?

Open More → Colour themes to view the unlock progress and choose an available palette. Light and dark mode are available for every unlocked theme, including both starting themes.

How do I send feedback or report a bug?

Use the Send feedback link at the top of this page. Describe the screen and steps to reproduce; screenshots help.

Privacy & Safety

What stays on your device, when a connection is made, and how to protect your collection.

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Your journal is local by default

Restaurant records, reviews, scores, dishes, dates, places, photographs, spending, lists and recovery snapshots are stored in this browser's IndexedDB database. Profile details, drafts, preferences and backup reminders are also stored in browser local storage. Optional online accounts also support deliberately published restaurant entries, friendship management and related safety records. The journal has no automatic synchronisation, public feed or advertising system; optional friends-only sharing is described below.

Only the selected previewed restaurant fields are intentionally uploaded when you choose to publish; receipts, photographs, spending and the rest of the journal remain local. Internet access to load the application still exposes technical connection data to the hosting infrastructure.

Operator and contact details — publication incomplete

Service name: Recolleat. Contact email: recolleat.paramount107@passmail.net. Legal operator's full name and any legally required establishment address: not yet supplied. These details still require verification and publication where applicable. The optional feedback form is hosted by Google and is not a substitute for a legal contact address.

If the operator is a data controller for hosting records or other processing, the controller identity and a working privacy contact must be stated here before publication. No operator identity has been invented.

What information is processed and why

Journal content: restaurant names, approximate locations you type, photos, ratings, dishes, notes, dates, price and payment fields, plus profile details. The purpose is to save and display your private journal and calculate your rankings, Palate Code, milestones, trends and recaps. You choose what to enter; there is no requirement to enter a real name or payment information.

Camera and receipts: camera/gallery access starts only when you choose a photograph. The receipt preview uses a temporary browser image URL. If your browser exposes the TextDetector feature, optical character recognition is attempted on-device; otherwise you can paste text copied through another app, which has its own privacy terms. Recognised lines may contain card fragments or other personal information: review them before saving. Selected dishes and prices can be saved into the local journal; Recolleat does not automatically save the original receipt image as part of this workflow.

Location pictures: You can choose a gallery image or paste a copied image for a location bubble. The selected image is compressed and stored in this browser in the local locations database, and is shared visually between restaurants using that location label. Recolleat does not automatically upload the picture. If you select Search Google Images, your browser opens Google and sends the location name in the search query; Google can process your connection information and is governed by its own privacy terms. Recolleat does not automatically retrieve or embed Google search results. Be mindful of image rights before reusing third-party pictures.

Automated insights: journal statistics and preferences are derived locally from entries and do not make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects. They are not medical, nutritional or financial assessments.

Who may receive connection information

Optional Google restaurant matching sends the search phrase and connection information to Google Maps when you explicitly search. Only the confirmed Google Place ID is retained in your local journal; other search results are not saved. You may deliberately include the identifier in a shared entry. Matching is currently disabled until configured. See Google Privacy Policy and Google Maps terms.

Optional restaurant discovery: searching your saved journal is local. If you choose Search Google Maps, Google receives the search text and normal connection information. Recolleat does not request your precise location or send the journal to Google. Google’s own settings and privacy notice govern the external Maps service.

Website hosting: this edition is deployed on a Cloudflare Workers-hosted address. Cloudflare and network intermediaries can receive technical connection information, such as Internet Protocol addresses, request metadata and security logs, when the site is accessed. Whether the account enables additional analytics, the exact retention period, subprocessors and international transfer safeguards must be confirmed by the site operator.

Optional Google Fonts: no Google Fonts request is made by default in this release. If you explicitly enable it below, your browser requests font resources from Google; Google can receive connection information such as your Internet Protocol address. You may disable subsequent font requests in the app’s Privacy & Safety settings at any time. Disabling this setting does not retract any network request already completed.

Feedback: selecting the external Google Forms feedback link takes you to a third-party site. Information you submit there is handled under Google's policies and the form owner's arrangements. No restaurant or journal data is sent there automatically.

Recolleat has no advertising identifiers or marketing email collection in this release (account email is used for authentication, not marketing). Optional Google Place ID matching is disabled until configured. The friends-only feed contains deliberately published entries. This is based on the distributed application code; hosting/account configurations must be checked separately.

Cookies, browser storage and choices

Recolleat uses IndexedDB, local storage, a service worker and Cache Storage to run your requested offline journal, retain edits, remember appearance settings and keep the application available offline. Local storage also records your optional font and following preferences and, if enabled, the account refresh token. Session storage retains a temporary authentication verifier and pending invite. The app does not create advertising or analytics cookies. We have not verified whether the hosting platform adds its own cookies or other tracking technologies.

Under United Kingdom rules, browser storage is also a storage-and-access technology; it does not have to be called a cookie to be regulated. Functionally necessary storage and qualifying appearance preferences may rely on relevant exceptions; other non-exempt technologies require appropriate choice or consent.

Optional online font requests are disabled by default.

For processing for which the operator is a controller and United Kingdom data protection law applies, the proposed assessment is: providing user-requested journal functionality may rely on performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b)); necessary site operation and protection against abuse may rely on legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f), subject to a balancing assessment); optional Google Fonts requests are made only following your affirmative choice (consent, Article 6(1)(a), where applicable). Account authentication and requested friends-only publishing are proposed as necessary to deliver the optional service; abuse handling and safety moderation require a documented lawful basis and balancing assessment where applicable. Deliberate publication is a user control, not a blanket consent to unrelated processing. These bases require a final assessment by the identified operator and are not a verified representation of Cloudflare's own processing.

Technical data may be transferred outside the United Kingdom depending on hosting and optional third-party services. The specific destinations, adequacy arrangements and appropriate safeguards have not been confirmed. These must be documented by the operator before a compliant public launch. No automated profiling is used to make legally significant decisions.

Retention, backups and security limitations

Journal data remains in this browser until you edit or delete it, clear site storage or lose access to that browser profile. A local recovery point resides on the same device and is not an off-device backup. The JSON export can contain your full journal, photographs, profile and spending information. Exported JSON is not encrypted by Recolleat; protect it, especially on shared devices, email and cloud drives.

Clearing app storage or uninstalling an installed web app can remove entries permanently. Export regularly and verify the downloaded file separately. Backups stored outside the app and any hosting logs are not deleted by Recolleat's local erase function. Hosting log retention remains unverified pending an operator-level review.

Camera/photo permissions are governed by your browser and device. Local storage is not guaranteed to be private from someone who can access your unlocked device or browser profile. Avoid recording payment card details and personal data about other people unless appropriate.

You can access, correct, export and erase journal entries using the app. Where data protection law applies to processing by an identified controller, other rights may include access, correction, restriction, erasure, portability, objection and withdrawal of consent, depending on the lawful basis and circumstances. Your right to object to processing based on legitimate interests can be exercised by contacting the controller using the published contact email. You can turn off optional Google Fonts in the app's Privacy & Safety settings. The operator must also provide a working data-protection complaints channel, acknowledge complaints within 30 days, investigate and explain the outcome, as required by the law effective June 2026. Send privacy requests or complaints to recolleat.paramount107@passmail.net. The operator must maintain an effective response procedure and complete any remaining legally required identity disclosures.

You may complain to the United Kingdom Information Commissioner's Office. Recolleat is not specifically designed for children. Access is not age-verified. If children are likely to use this service, the operator must assess and satisfy the applicable Children's code obligations before launch.

Online accounts, friends and sharing — development release

Recolleat is local-first. When you create an online account, Supabase processes your email, authentication data, display name, invitation code, friendships, blocked accounts and safety records. Google sign-in processes basic account identity, not contacts or journal history. Apple sign-in is included but not activated; ordinary email delivery is still restricted during development. The primary Supabase project is configured in London, United Kingdom; providers may process technical, support or other data internationally. See Supabase privacy information and Google privacy information.

Sharing is optional and private by default. In My Profile → Sharing settings, choose one restaurant and preview the exact information before publishing. Its name, your online display name and publication date are always included; you may choose location and confirmed Google Place ID, overall rating, selected dish-rating list (up to 30 rated dishes), and written review. No photographs, receipts, visit dates, spending, collections, or local profile picture are uploaded by this sharing feature. The feed is chronological, filtered by your local following preferences. All currently accepted friends, including those accepted later, can see published entries unless blocked, suspended or sharing is paused. Friends can view your online name and visible shared entries, not the remainder of your journal. No public feed, direct messages, stranger discovery or comments exist.

Pause sharing to hide all your entries, resume to make them visible again, or stop sharing an individual entry to delete its server copy. Removing or blocking a friend prevents further server access by that account; previously viewed information or screenshots cannot be recalled. Local edits or local erasure do not automatically change or delete shared copies. Deleting the online account deletes the online profile, friendships and shared entries; your local journal is separate. Account reports, shared-entry report reasons, moderation decisions and audit records may remain with account or entry links removed where applicable. Reports can be reviewed by authorised moderators, including the reported shared text while available; the private journal is not accessible through the moderation feature. Final safety record retention and operational procedures must be confirmed before release.

Invite links carry a private invitation code in the URL fragment; opening a link does not automatically create a request. The code is stored briefly in browser session storage until sent or dismissed. Share links only with intended people; you can replace your code. Keep me signed in stores a refresh token in local storage; without it the session is held in memory. A temporary sign-in verifier is stored in session storage. Avoid persistent sign-in on shared devices. Contact safety and privacy support for reports, complaints, appeals or data requests. This release is for controlled development testing, not public onboarding; legal operator, retention, international transfers and safety procedures remain to be verified.

Your local data controls

Export before deleting. Erasure affects Recolleat's journal and local profile in this browser, not shared entries, online accounts, previously downloaded files, browser-managed offline application files or hosting logs. Manage online entries separately in My Profile.

Logging, scoring, presentation and archived categories

Recolleat records a restaurant and visit together when you use Add. A food rating is required for completed Quick Log entries. Optional dish names, dish scores, photographs and a meal value (the value of food and drink attributable to you, not the whole table) are stored locally. The “Someone paid for my meal” choice records that you personally paid £0; otherwise personal payment is entered separately and may be unknown. This is an estimate, not a bank-verified transaction. Older visit entries with distinct personal-payment amounts are retained unless their meal value or payment status is changed.

Scoring weights and the cuisine ranking method (mean or median grand-list position) are saved in local preferences and included in new exported backups. Both options calculate on the device and are applied to displayed restaurant or cuisine rankings, respectively. Changing them recalculates displayed rankings but does not rewrite original food, value or service ratings. Older dish category information remains in existing records and backups for compatibility even though category books and category-entry controls are hidden. Graph data is not separately uploaded.

Photographs selected while adding a visit or dish are processed on-device into journal media and may be included in exported backups. My Profile displays any locally saved profile picture; it does not upload that picture. Optional accounts store identity, friend connections and explicitly shared text or ratings separately; there is no automatic journal cloud synchronisation or external journal image upload.
